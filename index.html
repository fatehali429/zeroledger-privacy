Privacy Policy

ZeroLedger
Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ZeroLedger Android application (package name com.zeroledger.app) handles information. ZeroLedger is developed by Fatehali Sunasara (HT) - Meta ("we").

Information you enter

ZeroLedger lets you create organization names, Ledger names and descriptions, phone numbers, transaction amounts, transaction types, notes, and dates. This information may contain personal or financial information about you or other people. It is stored in the app's local database on your device and is used to provide Ledger, balance, search, and report features.

Information collection and sharing

ZeroLedger does not require an account and does not send the information you enter to us. The app does not include advertising or analytics services and does not request network access. We do not sell or share your information.

You may choose to create a PDF report and save or share it using Android's system interfaces, or to create an encrypted backup file at a location you choose. Those actions can make information available to the storage provider, app, or person you select. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices, not this policy. We do not receive a copy through these actions.

Backups and reports

Backups are created only when you initiate the action. A backup file is encrypted with the passphrase you provide. ZeroLedger does not save that passphrase, operate a cloud backup service, or perform automatic background backups. Keep the passphrase safe; a backup cannot be restored without it. Backup files saved outside the app remain wherever you saved them until you remove them.

PDF reports may contain the Ledger and transaction details selected for the report. Review the report and choose its save or share destination carefully.

App lock and security

If you enable app lock, Android handles the device biometric or screen-lock verification. ZeroLedger receives only the authentication result; it does not access or store your biometric information. App lock restricts access through the app but does not encrypt the live database. The live database is stored locally and is not encrypted by ZeroLedger. Protect your device with its screen lock and do not share exported reports or backup files carelessly.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app on your device until you delete them, delete their organization, or uninstall the app. You can delete records and organizations within ZeroLedger. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, but does not delete backup files or copies of reports that you saved or shared elsewhere. Remove those files from their chosen locations and contact recipients directly if you want their copies deleted.

Children's privacy

ZeroLedger is a general-purpose Ledger utility and is not designed to collect information from children. Because records stay on the device and are not transmitted to us, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's information practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published at the policy URL provided in the app's Google Play listing.
