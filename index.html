Privacy Policy

ZeroLedger
Effective date: November 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ZeroLedger Android application (package name com.zeroledger.app) handles information. ZeroLedger is developed by Fatehali Sunasara (HT) - Meta ("we").

Information you enter

ZeroLedger lets you create organization names, Ledger names and descriptions, transaction amounts, transaction types, notes, and dates. Records may contain personal or financial information about you or other people. They are stored in the app's local database on your device and used to provide Ledger, balance, search, and report features. App preferences, such as your selected currency and app-lock setting, are also stored on the device.

Network access and Google Play billing

ZeroLedger does not require an account. Its core Ledger features work offline, and the app does not send the records you enter to the developer. The app uses network access for Google Play Billing to look up the unlock product, check purchases, and process an unlock. If you enable automatic backups, Android's document provider for the folder you select is used to write encrypted backup files there. If you select Google Drive, Google handles Drive access and storage under Google's own privacy terms. ZeroLedger does not receive your payment credentials and does not operate a backup server. The app does not include advertising or analytics services.

We do not sell your Ledger records. If you choose to create, save, or share a PDF report, or save a backup using Android's file picker, the information in that file is made available to the storage provider, app, or person you select. A provider you choose may store files in its own cloud service. Their handling of that information is governed by their own privacy practices; we do not receive a copy through these actions.

Backups and reports

You can create a manual encrypted backup or, if you enable the premium automatic-backup feature, select a folder (for example, in Google Drive) for daily backups. Automatic backups are encrypted on your device with the passphrase you provide before they are written to the selected folder. Android may defer scheduled work depending on device and network conditions. Up to seven automatic backup files are kept in the selected folder; older automatic backup files may be deleted as new ones are created. Manual backup files are not automatically deleted.

To run scheduled backups without asking for the passphrase each time, ZeroLedger stores it locally in Android Keystore-protected app storage. The selected folder reference and backup status are also stored on the device. This information is not sent to ZeroLedger's servers; ZeroLedger does not operate a backup server or require a ZeroLedger account. Google handles access to and storage in the selected Drive folder under Google's privacy terms. Keep the passphrase safe: it is required to restore backups, including on another device. Removing the app or turning off automatic backups does not delete backup files already saved in Drive. Files remain there until you delete them.

PDF reports may contain the Ledger and transaction details selected for the report. Review the report and choose its save or share destination carefully.

App lock and security

If you enable app lock, Android handles the device biometric or screen-lock verification. ZeroLedger receives only the authentication result; it does not access or store your biometric information. App lock restricts access through the app but does not encrypt the live database. The live database is stored locally and is not encrypted by ZeroLedger. Protect your device with its screen lock and do not share exported reports or backup files carelessly.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain in the app on your device until you delete them, delete their organization, or uninstall the app. You can delete records and organizations within ZeroLedger. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, but does not delete backup files or copies of reports that you saved or shared elsewhere. Remove those files from their chosen locations and contact recipients directly if you want their copies deleted.

Children's privacy

ZeroLedger is a general-purpose Ledger utility and is not designed to collect information from children. Because records stay on the device and are not transmitted to us, we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app's information practices change. The current version and its effective date will be published at the policy URL provided in the app's Google Play listing.
